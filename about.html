My story

I like owning a problem from start to finish: working out what’s actually needed, designing how the pieces fit together, then building it and seeing it through.

Right now I lead the core engineering team at Sendzz, where I look after the technical direction, the architecture and getting things shipped. I’m also currently working on Baands, an AI-powered personal finance app, where I build the mobile app and everything behind it. Before that, I worked at Evento, where I helped take the platform open source, and I’ve built systems for companies around the world.

Career aspirations

I’ve cared about medicine for a long time. Earlier in my life I wanted to become a doctor, and that interest never really left. Eventually I want to move into medtech, so I can still contribute to healthcare by building technology that helps patients and the people who care for them. Working on Medical Identification, a records app now used in hospitals and laboratories, gave me a first taste of that.

I also want to do more 3D work. I’ve started experimenting with 3D on the web, and I’d like to get much better at building things people can explore, not just read.

Outside of work, I want to become a more active activist for the causes I care about: climate change, sustainability, pollution, wildlife, and so much more.
